THE PROCESS

How ASE works

A transparent marketplace connecting AI strategy builders, investors, and the market — with independent verification at every step.

01
BUILD

Submit your strategy

Builders submit AI trading agents to ASE. Each listing follows a structured evaluation process designed to reduce self-reported performance and make strategies genuinely comparable.

02
VERIFY

Validate the record

We maintain a consistent, audit-friendly trade ledger — plus risk analytics and full disclosures. Investors can evaluate agents on more than just marketing-grade returns.

03
TRADE

Allocate with confidence

When the platform opens for transactions, investors will find clearer terms, clearer risk, and clearer performance than any typical strategy marketplace. Investments involve risk.

    What you should know

    Transparency starts before you invest. Honest answers to the questions that matter most.

    An AI trading agent is an autonomous program that makes trading decisions based on market data — using ML models, statistical signals, or systematic rules. On ASE, every listing is designed to include a clear methodology statement, risk analytics, and a transparent performance record so investors can actually evaluate the agent rather than just trust a headline number.
    ASE is currently in development and is not a registered broker-dealer, investment adviser, licensed exchange, or alternative trading system. We are working with experienced regulatory counsel to determine the appropriate structure before facilitating any investment transactions. Joining the waitlist is not an investment and confers no legal rights.
    They may be, depending on how the product is structured. If a product involves people investing money with an expectation of profit based primarily on someone else's efforts, it is commonly treated as a security in the US. We will be explicit about eligibility requirements, required disclosures, and any registrations or exemptions before any offering is made.
    Investing in trading strategies involves significant risk, including possible loss of all capital. Key risks include market volatility, model failure, slippage and execution issues, liquidity constraints, and operational or platform risk. AI systems can behave unexpectedly in novel market conditions. Past performance does not predict future results. Only invest what you can afford to lose.
