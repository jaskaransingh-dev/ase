QUESTIONS

Frequently asked

What exactly is an "AI trading agent" on ASE? +
An AI trading agent is an autonomous software program that makes financial trading decisions based on market data, machine learning models, or rule-based logic. On ASE, each agent is a distinct entity with a performance record, a risk profile, and tokenized equity that can be bought and sold like a stock. Agents trade real or simulated markets, and their returns are verified before any equity can be listed.
Is this regulated? Is my money safe? +
ASE is being built with regulatory compliance at its core. We are pursuing appropriate licensing in relevant jurisdictions, with KYC/AML screening at onboarding. All listed agents go through a mandatory audit and live paper-trading verification period before investor capital is accepted. Like any investment, past performance does not guarantee future results — all investment carries risk.
How do agent developers make money? +
Developers can earn by deploying agents on ASE. Earnings are based on agent performance and investor participation. Details will be provided at launch.
What markets do agents trade? +
At launch, ASE will support agents trading US equities, major forex pairs, crypto spot markets, and futures. We plan to expand to commodities and international equities in subsequent phases. Each agent's permitted market universe is disclosed at listing.
Can I withdraw my investment at any time? +
ASE operates a secondary market where investors can sell their agent equity stakes to other buyers. Liquidity is not guaranteed for all agents at all times, but the secondary market is designed to provide meaningful exit liquidity for the majority of listed agents. Specific liquidity details will vary by agent.

Risk Disclosure
Important — Please read carefully before investing

Investment Risk

All investments in AI trading agents carry significant risk. You may lose some or all of your invested capital. The value of agent equity can go up or down, and past performance is not indicative of future results.

AI and Algorithmic Risk

AI trading agents can malfunction, overfit historical data, or fail in market conditions not seen during development. Market conditions change in ways that may render previously profitable strategies unprofitable.

Market Risk

Agents trade in financial markets subject to volatility, liquidity risk, regulatory changes, and systemic events (market crashes, flash crashes, etc.) that can cause rapid and significant losses.

Liquidity Risk

While ASE operates a secondary market, there is no guarantee of liquidity for any agent equity position. You may not be able to sell your position at a desired price or time.

Regulatory Risk

The regulatory environment for AI, fintech, and digital assets is evolving. Changes in regulation could affect ASE's operations, agent listings, or your ability to trade.

Counterparty Risk

There is risk that developers may cease operations, that agents may be delisted, or that the ASE platform itself may experience operational issues.

Only Invest What You Can Afford to Lose

Never invest money you cannot afford to lose. Diversify your investments and consult a qualified financial advisor before making investment decisions.
